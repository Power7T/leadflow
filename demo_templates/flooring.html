Services

Flooring Services We Offer

From hardwood refinishing to full commercial installations — we handle every project with precision, speed, and lasting craftsmanship.

🪵

Hardwood Floor Installation

Solid and engineered hardwood in every species, stain, and finish. Expert installation with proper subfloor preparation and acclimation for long-lasting results.

💧

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

100% waterproof, durable, and realistic-looking LVP for kitchens, bathrooms, and basements. The most popular flooring choice for modern homes.

🏛️

Tile & Stone Installation

Porcelain, ceramic, natural stone, and large-format tile installed to perfection. Kitchens, bathrooms, entryways — done with precision leveling and tight grout lines.

🛋️

Carpet & Area Rugs

Plush, textured, and performance carpets for bedrooms, offices, and basements. Expert power stretching installation for a lifetime of smooth, wrinkle-free results.

✨

Floor Refinishing & Restoration

Bring worn, scratched hardwood floors back to life. Sand, stain, and finish to look better than new — at a fraction of replacement cost.

🏢

Commercial Flooring

High-traffic solutions for offices, retail, restaurants, and multi-unit properties. LVT, commercial carpet, polished concrete, and epoxy floor systems.
