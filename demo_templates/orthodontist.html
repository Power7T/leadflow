Treatments

Orthodontic Treatment Options

From traditional braces to virtually invisible aligners — we have the right solution for every smile, age, and lifestyle.

🦷

Traditional Metal Braces

The gold standard of orthodontics. Modern braces are smaller, more comfortable, and faster than ever — and still the most effective for complex cases.

✨

Invisalign Clear Aligners

Nearly invisible, removable aligners that work for most teens and adults. Straighten your teeth without anyone knowing — eat what you want, brush normally.

👶

Teen Braces & Early Treatment

We specialize in Phase 1 treatment for children 7–10 to intercept issues early and avoid more complex treatment later. Palate expanders and interceptive ortho.

🔄

Retainers & Post-Treatment

Custom Hawley and clear retainers to protect your investment. Nighttime wear keeps your smile perfect for life after active treatment ends.

🎨

Smile Design Consultation

Digital smile simulation before treatment begins. See your projected result, plan the journey, and start with confidence and clear expectations.

💎

Teeth Whitening

Professional-grade whitening to complete your smile transformation post-treatment. Take-home trays and in-office options for dramatically brighter results.
