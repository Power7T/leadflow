FAILOVER SCENARIO 1 (VIVO PHONE DROPS / ADB CRASHES):
- Firestick stops catching Vivo heartbeats.
- Firestick promotes itself back to LEADER.
- Firestick spins up `server.py` and CRM tasks using its standby DB.
- *(Note: IG DMing skips since Firestick can't do local UI execution)*

FAILOVER SCENARIO 2 (BOTH VIVO & FIRESTICK CRASH / POWER OUTAGE):
- Mac detects missed heartbeats from BOTH Android devices in Cloudflare KV.
- Mac promotes itself to LEADER.
- Mac spins up `server.py` and `scheduler.py` handling DB / emails / sync.
- Mac can optionally try to reach Vivo via remote Wi-Fi ADB to force a restart.
